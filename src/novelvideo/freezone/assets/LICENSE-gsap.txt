GSAP 3.15.0 (gsap.min.js) — vendored for offline HyperFrames rendering
=====================================================================

Why vendored: the HyperFrames producer renders each generated project headlessly
with zero outbound network at runtime. Its own guidance (probeStage diagnostics)
is to "bundle GSAP locally in your project instead of using a CDN <script src>".
This is the exact GSAP version that `@hyperframes/core` depends on (gsap ^3.15.0)
and that the HyperFrames registry vendors, so the composition runtime matches the
engine's own baseline.
